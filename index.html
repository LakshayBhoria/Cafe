Our Story

Where Sonipat comes to eat, talk, and unwind.

The Rock Café started with a simple idea — a neighbourhood spot that's easy to love. Warm booths, a menu that swings from butter chicken to penne arrabbiata, and weekend nights where local musicians take the small stage.

Whether it's a study break, a family dinner, or a birthday that needs a bit of music — we've kept a table and a mic on for you.

Multi-Cuisine Kitchen North Indian, Chinese, Italian, biryani & fast food — all under one roof.
Live Music Weekends Acoustic sets and open mic nights every Friday & Saturday.
Built for Groups Indoor booths and an open-air deck for parties up to 20.
